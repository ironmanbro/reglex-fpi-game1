    How a file works

    1. Read the facts on the case folder.
    2. Make your call. A right call is stamped Cleared, a wrong one Escalated.
    3. Read why, why every other option fails, and the exact regulation.
    4. At the end of the round, review every call you made.
    • RookieStraight reads of the rules. 100 points.
    • DetectiveTwo rules colliding. 200 points.
    • InspectorThe fine print that trips up experienced desks. 300 points.
